Petition Decisions in Reexamination Proceedings

Every petition filed in a tracked ex parte reexamination proceeding — any party, any subject — with the relief requested, the opposing paper (if any), and the Office's decision, each linked to the underlying document. Filter by type of relief.

Parties in reexamination petition the Office for many kinds of relief — to vacate or terminate the proceeding, reconsider the order, extend time, strike papers, revive, or invoke supervisory authority. This page collects the full petition trail from each proceeding's file wrapper: the petition (RXPET./PET.OP), any opposition (RXOPPPET), and the Office's decision (RXPT*). The relief requested is read from the decision itself, which conventionally recites what the petition asked for; because a single petition commonly asks for several things, relief is multi-label and the substantive ask is highlighted.

Statistics key on the merits. The most common shape in this corpus is a combined petition in which the Office grants a procedural waiver (37 CFR 1.183, of rules such as 1.515(a) or 1.540) so that it can reach a request to terminate the reexamination under 35 U.S.C. § 325(d) — and then dismisses that request. Granting the waiver is not a win, so the grant rate here counts only the disposition of the substantive relief; the waiver leg is noted separately as “(waiver granted)”. Petitions are matched to decisions by filing order; a decision whose petition paper is not separately on file appears as a decision-only row. Collection updates with the same daily scan that watches each proceeding for its certificate, and stops when the proceeding concludes.
