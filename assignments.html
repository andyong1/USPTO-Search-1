Assignment chain

Every assignment recorded at the USPTO for a patent or application, newest first, with the assignors, assignees, execution and recording dates, and a link to each recorded document. The current owner is taken from the most recent record that actually transfers title — security interests, releases, liens and licences are shown but never treated as ownership, because the “assignee” on those is a lender or collateral agent.

Assignments are recorded against the underlying application, not against a reexamination control number — enter the patent or its application, not a 90/ or 96/ control number.
