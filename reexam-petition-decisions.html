Petition Decisions in Reexamination Proceedings

Every petition filed in a tracked ex parte reexamination proceeding — any party, any subject — with the opposing paper (if any) and the Office's decision (granted or dismissed), each linked to the underlying document.

Parties in reexamination petition the Office for many kinds of relief — to vacate or reconsider the order, strike papers, extend time, revive, or invoke supervisory authority. This page collects the full petition trail from each proceeding's file wrapper: the petition (RXPET./PET.OP), any opposition (RXOPPPET), and the Office's decision, whose outcome (granted / dismissed) is taken from the USPTO's own document codes. Petitions are matched to decisions by filing order; a decision whose petition paper is not separately on file appears as a decision-only row. Collection updates automatically with the same scan that watches each proceeding for its certificate, and stops when the proceeding concludes.
